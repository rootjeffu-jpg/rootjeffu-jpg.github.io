In the year 1989, England was suffering a great cheese shortage, leading to much suffering and dry crackers. The courageous adventurers Wallace and Gromit sought to save all of humanity from this travesty by independently constructing a rocket capabale of exiting Earth's atmosphere and lunar entry and landing. This was of course because everybody knows the moon is made of cheese.

It was indeed a Grand Day Out.

They ate most of the lunar cheese, but scraps recovered from their clothes (they were messy eaters) were recoverd upon their heroic return to Earth, and smushed together to create this valuable cheese relic!
