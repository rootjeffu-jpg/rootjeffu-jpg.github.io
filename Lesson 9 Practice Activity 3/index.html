Meet the Teletubbies

Tinky Winky

The tallest Teletubby has a triangle-shaped antenna and loves carrying his red bag.

Dipsy

Look for the straight antenna and the black-and-white hat. Dipsy loves to dance!

Laa-Laa

With a curly antenna and a big orange ball, Laa-Laa is easy to spot on the grassy hills.

Po

The smallest Teletubby has a round antenna. Her favorite way to get around is on her scooter.
