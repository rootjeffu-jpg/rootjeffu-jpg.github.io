Is Artificial Intelligence Methods and Applications Right for Me?

Do you enjoy programming, working through math problems, or figuring out why a computer made a particular prediction? In this major, you'll explore how AI systems work and how their decisions affect people.

What You'll Study

You'll use programming, mathematics, and statistics to investigate how machines learn from data. Courses also explore how computers represent knowledge, choose among possible solutions, and support human decisions.

Supporting Courses

Technical supporting courses let you explore topics such as data mining, network analytics, and applied statistics. Application supporting courses let you choose a focus area and take a sequence of courses in that field.
