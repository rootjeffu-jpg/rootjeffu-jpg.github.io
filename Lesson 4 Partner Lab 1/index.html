Knot Tying

Knots are essential tools in emergency situations, providing the means to secure loads, create shelters, and perform rescue operations. Learning to tie various knots can significantly improve your chances of survival in a crisis.

We're going to cover three essential knots:

  1. The Bowline Knot
  2. The Square Knot
  3. The Clove Hitch

Bowline Knot

The Bowline Knot is known for creating a secure loop at the end of a rope. It is easy to tie and untie, even after being subjected to a load. This knot is often used in rescue situations and for securing objects.

Square Knot

The Square Knot is a simple knot used to join two ropes of equal thickness. It is commonly used for securing loads and creating makeshift slings. While it is easy to tie, it should not be used under heavy load.

Clove Hitch

The Clove Hitch is a versatile knot used to secure a rope to a post or pole. It is easy to tie and untie, making it ideal for temporary anchoring needs.
