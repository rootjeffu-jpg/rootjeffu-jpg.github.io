Survival Kit Preparation

A survival kit should contain the supplies you need to stay safe, informed, and comfortable for at least several days. Store it somewhere easy to reach and check it twice a year.

Essential Supplies

  • Water: at least one gallon per person per day
  • Nonperishable food and a manual can opener
  • Flashlight, extra batteries, and a battery-powered radio
  • First-aid supplies and necessary medications
  • Phone charger or backup battery
  • Blankets, extra clothing, and sturdy shoes
  • Copies of identification, emergency contacts, and insurance information

Make It Personal

Add supplies for children, older adults, pets, and anyone with medical or accessibility needs. Include hygiene items, masks, cash, and a whistle. Label the kit clearly and keep a smaller version in your car or workplace.
